# INWEB — 30-Second Demo Video Script

> Ready-to-shoot storyboard for your Play Store promo video, YouTube ad,
> or social media reel. Timing is precise for a 30-second cut, but
> everything scales cleanly to 45s or 60s.

---

## 🎥 Format

- **Duration:** 30 seconds
- **Resolution:** 1080×1920 (portrait) OR 1920×1080 (landscape)
- **Frame rate:** 30 fps
- **Audio:** Upbeat lo-fi with a light Middle-Eastern flavour, ~90 BPM
- **Voice-over:** Optional — text overlays alone tell the story
- **On-screen language:** English by default; localise to Bangla/Arabic
  for regional cuts

---

## 🎞 Shot-by-shot storyboard

### 🎬 Shot 1 — Hook (0:00 → 0:03)
**Visual:**
Hand holding a phone. Screen shows a plain desktop with the INWEB logo
fading in over a deep-green gradient background. Islamic geometric
pattern faintly animates in.

**Text overlay:**
> *"What if your phone was a web server?"*

**Sound:** Whoosh + soft chime.

---

### 🎬 Shot 2 — Reveal (0:03 → 0:06)
**Visual:**
Cut to phone screen: user taps INWEB icon. App opens to the dashboard
with a satisfying spring animation. Big green dot pulses, "Server
Stopped" fades to "Server Running", and the URL `http://localhost:8080`
slides in from the right.

**Text overlay:**
> **"INWEB — A Powerful Web Server that fits in your Pocket."**

**Sound:** Notification pop + subtle "you got mail" chime.

---

### 🎬 Shot 3 — Full Stack (0:06 → 0:10)
**Visual:**
Quick cut to Services screen. Three tiles cascade in one after another:
Nginx → PHP-FPM → MariaDB. Each tile's dot turns green as it appears.

**Text overlay:**
> *"Nginx · PHP · MariaDB — all bundled"*

**Sound:** Three quick chime hits, one per service.

---

### 🎬 Shot 4 — Pro Editor (0:10 → 0:14)
**Visual:**
Cut to editor screen. Somebody types PHP code — `<?php echo "Hello,
World!"; ?>` — and syntax highlighting kicks in dynamically as they
type. Zoom into the colours.

**Text overlay:**
> *"Pro editor · 12 languages · 4 themes"*

**Sound:** Soft mechanical-keyboard typing SFX.

---

### 🎬 Shot 5 — Share Anywhere (0:14 → 0:18)
**Visual:**
Cut to Share & QR screen. QR code renders instantly. Cut to a second
phone (or laptop) that scans it — a WordPress site loads. Split-screen
briefly showing "your phone" and "another device".

**Text overlay:**
> *"Share via QR · LAN · Cloudflare Tunnel"*

**Sound:** Camera-scan beep + soft page-load whoosh.

---

### 🎬 Shot 6 — Islamic APIs (0:18 → 0:22)
**Visual:**
Cut to a browser tab loading `localhost:8080/api/prayer-times.php`.
JSON response scrolls with prayer times. Cut to a small Qibla compass
animation overlaid on the response.

**Text overlay:**
> *"🕌 Built-in Islamic APIs — offline"*

**Sound:** Soft *adhan*-inspired chime (respectful, brief).

---

### 🎬 Shot 7 — 1-Click Install (0:22 → 0:26)
**Visual:**
Frameworks screen. User taps "Install" on WordPress card. Progress
bar zips to 100%. Cut immediately to WordPress installation page
loading in the browser.

**Text overlay:**
> *"1-click WordPress · Laravel · Bootstrap"*

**Sound:** Progress-bar hum + satisfying "done" pop.

---

### 🎬 Shot 8 — Close (0:26 → 0:30)
**Visual:**
INWEB logo centered on the deep-green background, wordmark appears
below with tagline "A Powerful Web Server that fits in your Pocket." Small
Play Store badge fades in at the bottom.

**Text overlay:**
> **"INWEB"**
> *"Download free on Google Play"*

**Sound:** Final chime, fades to silence with a subtle heartbeat pulse.

## 🎼 Suggested music tracks (royalty-free)

- **YouTube Audio Library:** search for "Middle Eastern Ambient Chill"
- **Pixabay:** "Arabic Lofi" tracks (~90 BPM)
- **Free Music Archive:** Bensound's "Sunny" (upbeat, dev-vibe alt.)

Avoid copyrighted music for the Play Store promo — Play Console rejects
listings with third-party music.

---

## 📽 Editing checklist

- [ ] Text overlays readable for at least 1.5s each
- [ ] Every UI shot is real INWEB, not a mockup
- [ ] No fictional data (use "your name", "your project")
- [ ] Play Store logo at the end, not third-party stores
- [ ] Captions/subtitles enabled (accessibility + no-audio autoplay)
- [ ] Final 3s has a strong call-to-action
- [ ] Export at ≤ 100 MB for Play Console upload
