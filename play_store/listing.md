## 9. Sensitive-permissions justification

Play Console asks for these declarations when you upload:

| Permission | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE` | Keeps the Nginx/PHP/MariaDB processes alive while the app is backgrounded — the whole point of a local server. |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Optional auto-start; user must enable in Settings. |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Shows the silent ongoing notification with a Stop action while running. |
| `WAKE_LOCK` | Prevents CPU from throttling while serving requests. |

We do NOT request: location, contacts, storage broadcast, SMS, camera, mic.
